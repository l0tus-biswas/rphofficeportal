AGENT PRODUCER AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between {{ branding.appName }} ("Company") and the applicant ("Agent").

1. APPOINTMENT

The Company hereby appoints the Agent as an independent contractor to market and sell insurance products on behalf of the Company and its carrier partners.

2. AGENT RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Maintain all required state insurance licenses in good standing
  • Comply with all applicable insurance laws and regulations
  • Follow Company policies, procedures, and ethical guidelines
  • Submit accurate and complete applications
  • Provide excellent customer service to clients
3. COMPENSATION

Agent shall receive commissions on policies sold according to the Company's commission schedule. Commissions are subject to policy activation and premium payment.

4. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

Agent is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Company. Agent is responsible for all taxes, insurance, and business expenses.

5. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice. The Company may terminate immediately for cause, including fraud or misconduct.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Agent agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary information, client data, and business practices of the Company.

7. COMPLIANCE

Agent agrees to comply with all applicable federal and state laws, including but not limited to insurance regulations, anti-fraud statutes, and consumer protection laws.

By signing below, Agent acknowledges reading and agreeing to all terms and conditions of this Agreement.
